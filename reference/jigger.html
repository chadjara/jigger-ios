      How conversions work

      Costs are always calculated from exactly what you typed, using exact unit factors. When you switch between oz and mL, pours are shown using the bar convention of 1 oz ≈ 30 mL (1.5 oz shows as 45 mL), and switching never changes what you entered. Fluid ounces measure volume; “oz wt” measures weight.
