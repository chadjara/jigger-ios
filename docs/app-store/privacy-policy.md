# Jigger Cost Privacy Policy

_Effective: November 19, 2026_

Jigger Cost ("the app") is a drink cost calculator for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version

The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't connect to the internet.

## Information on your device

Recipes, saved ingredients, prices and settings you enter are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are included in your device's own backups (iCloud or computer) if you have those turned on, under Apple's terms. Deleting the app deletes this information. You can also delete all recipes and ingredients at any time under **Settings → Delete all data**.

## Files you choose to export or share

The app creates a file only when you ask it to:

- **Export backup** creates a JSON file of your recipes and ingredients.
- **Share PDF** and **Print** create a cost analysis sheet for a drink.

Where those files go is up to you (Files, AirDrop, Mail, a printer and so on), and the service you choose handles them under its own terms. The app never sends anything anywhere on its own.

## Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

## Children

The app is a business tool and isn't directed at children. It collects no information from anyone, including children.
