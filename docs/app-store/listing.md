## Version information

### Promotional text (170, can be changed any time without review)

Cost any cocktail to the cent in seconds. Set your target pour cost, get a suggested menu price, and see your margin before the drink hits the menu.

### Description (4000)

Jigger Cost is a fast, accurate drink cost calculator for bartenders, bar managers and owners. Enter what you paid for a bottle and how much goes into the drink, and Jigger shows the cost of every ingredient, the cost per drink, and what to charge.

BUILT FOR A BUSY SHIFT
• Live totals as you type, with no save button to find first
• One-tap pours (¼ oz to 3 oz, or 7.5 to 90 mL) and common bottle sizes from 50 mL to 1.75 L
• Switch between oz and mL at any time. What you entered never changes.
• Big, clear numbers on a dark charcoal and brass design that's easy to read behind the bar

ACCURATE TO THE CENT
• Exact unit conversions. Fluid ounces and ounces by weight are kept separate.
• Count and weight ingredients: half a lime, a 2 lb bag of sugar, a case of 40 limes
• Waste and spillage allowance, per recipe or per ingredient
• Garnish, napkins, straws and cups as additional costs
• Rounding happens only for display, so batches don't drift

PRICE WITH CONFIDENCE
• Suggested menu price from your target beverage cost %
• Enter your menu price to see beverage cost %, gross profit and gross margin
• Scale any recipe to 10, 25, 100 or any number of drinks
• Print or share a clean cost analysis PDF with an ingredient breakdown, your biggest cost driver, a price ladder at 18/20/22/25% and batch totals
• Print your whole recipe book (specs, costs and pricing at a glance) and a bar price list with cost per ounce

YOUR RECIPES, YOUR BOTTLES
• Save recipes, then search, duplicate, reorder ingredients, and delete with undo
• My Ingredients: update a bottle's price once and every recipe that uses it recalculates
• A custom price in one recipe is never overwritten
• Back up and restore with a simple JSON file

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your device and works offline.

Figures cover ingredient and listed additional costs only. They don't include labor, rent, taxes or overhead. The included Classic Margarita example uses illustrative prices.

## App Privacy (questionnaire)

- **Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?** No.
- Result: **Data Not Collected**. This matches the app's `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`: no tracking, no collected data, no network access.

## Age rating

Answer the questionnaire honestly. The relevant item is **Alcohol, Tobacco, or Drug Use or References**: the app costs alcoholic drinks but doesn't depict or encourage drinking. Choose the level you're comfortable defending; "Infrequent/Mild" and "Frequent/Intense" give different ratings. Every other item is "None".

## App Review information

- **Sign-in required:** No.
- **Notes for the reviewer:**

  > Jigger Cost is a drink cost calculator for bars and restaurants. No account or network connection is needed. On first launch, a Classic Margarita example with illustrative prices shows the calculator. Tap "Clear example" to start a new drink. Saved recipes, My Ingredients and Settings are in the tab bar (sidebar on iPad). "Share PDF" in the cost summary creates the cost analysis sheet.
